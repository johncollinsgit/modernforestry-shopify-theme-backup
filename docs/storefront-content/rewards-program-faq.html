What is Candle Cash?

Candle Cash is Modern Forestry reward credit. Sign in to Your Rewards with the account you use to shop to see your balance, available activities, and reward codes.

How do I earn Candle Cash?

Complete eligible activities in Your Rewards. Rewards are added after the activity is verified; activities that need a team review may show as pending first.

  • Join our email list: $5.
  • Sign up for text messages: $2.
  • Leave a verified Google review: $3.
  • Leave a verified product review through our supported review platform: $2.
  • Place your second order: $5.
  • Refer a friend who places a qualifying first order: $10. The qualifying referred friend receives $5 too.
  • Become an active Candle Club member: $2.
  • Complete an eligible Candle Club vote: $1.
  • Comment on a qualifying Instagram post or reel: $2 after our team verifies it.

Eligibility, availability, and completion limits are shown beside each activity in Your Rewards. Use the activity's link and instructions so we can verify it. Creating an account or following Instagram does not earn the old points bonuses.

How do I use Candle Cash online?

  1. Sign in and open Your Rewards.
  2. When you have at least $10 in available Candle Cash, redeem $10 for a reward code. You can have one open Candle Cash code at a time.
  3. Use the reward's apply button, or copy the code into the discount field at checkout.
  4. Check that the discount appears in your order summary before paying.

Candle Cash can only be redeemed once per customer per day (Eastern Time), with a daily reset at midnight Eastern Time. You can use up to $10 in Candle Cash per online order. A reward code is not a gift card or cash payment. The reward's current checkout eligibility and discount combination rules apply. Candle Cash is not applied automatically just because you are signed in.

How does RETAILOVER75 free shipping work?

Use RETAILOVER75 on eligible one-time retail orders of $75 or more shipped within the United States. The selected shipping rate must be $20 or less to qualify for free shipping.

Shipping rates over $20 are not covered by this code and receive no shipping discount. Review the delivery options offered at checkout and choose a cheaper eligible rate if one is available. Rates and availability depend on your order and delivery address.

Confirm that the shipping discount appears in your order summary before paying. If no eligible rate is available, contact our team for help.

How does Birthday Club work?

Save your birthday in Birthday Club to receive your annual $10 birthday coupon. The coupon is valid for 30 days and can be combined with eligible free shipping offers. Birthday Club shows your saved birthday, birthday reward status, and any usable birthday coupon.

The birthday coupon is separate from your Candle Cash balance. Saving a birthday does not add a new signup bonus. Existing rewards and previously earned balances stay on your account.

What happened to the old rewards points?

Your Rewards now shows reward credit in dollars. The old points tiers and rewards bubble instructions no longer describe the current program. Sign in to view your existing credit and available codes.

My balance or code is missing. What should I do?

Check that you are signed in with the same account used for your purchases and reward activities, then reopen Your Rewards. If an activity is pending, allow time for verification. If you still need help, contact our team before placing a second order. Include the email address on your account and the activity or code you are asking about.
